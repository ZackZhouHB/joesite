Have you ever wondered what new methods you could bring to the classroom? Here, I will explain several approaches I would implement if I were the teacher.

First, I would introduce creative teaching. For instance, I would allow students to not only sit under their desks but also stand on their chairs. This would provide freedom in classroom seating, allowing children to discover new angles and perspectives to explore the world. For writing activities, students would have the opportunity to write in various places, such as on tree bark, the ground, or anywhere they feel inspired. This approach would help them capture fresh ideas and enhance their imagination, thereby improving their writing content and vocabulary.

Next, I would try blended learning. This strategy combines face-to-face instruction from the teacher with online resources and assignments that students can choose. It helps children gain control over their learning process and build their capability to master online material independently.

Finally, I would utilize modern technology such as mobile apps to provide students with personalized learning recommendations. For example, I would offer AI-powered reading apps that has various materials like comic books, children's literature, and kids' magazines. Based on their choices and reading records, these apps can help identify each child's unique interests and current reading levels, allowing me to offer personalized learning content to each student.

In conclusion, I believe these methods would not only be highly effective in achieving creative teaching and making learning more enjoyable for students but also create a motivated and competitive environment in a modern classroom.
