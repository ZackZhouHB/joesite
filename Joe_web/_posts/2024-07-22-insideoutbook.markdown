Yhe two storyline book is about the mind inside the head of an 11 year-old girl named Riley. It talks about how the main 5 emotions Fear Anger Disgust Sadness and Joy together impact Riley’s reaction with a council inside her brain. The story is extremely engaging and emotional as it is really good at creating tension like Ryan Riley running away we losing joy and Sadness and in the end the problem got solved in an hearwarming way.

A list of other Disney book were piled up in my bookshelf. Dad bought them a year ago. Finishing "Inside Out" encouraged me to keep working on rest of them by next year hopefully. 

My favorite emotions in the book are Anger and Disgust because they were both cool and memorable. I remember some of the red emotion's actions and dialogue really showed how angry and mad he was, like he had bursting into flames and punching Fear, which I kept repeating at home.

Disgust had a bigger impact on me. I loved the anger and discuss and they will definitely stay in my head for sometime.

I recently watched the Pixar movie "Inside Out", and "Inside Out 2", if I would compare the book to the original film, I think the movie is better. I especially loved the dramatic piano of Riley and the peaceful music at the beginning of the movie when she was born.

Overall "Inside Out" is a well-structured and engating story that tells about growth and emotions, known for its creativity of Riley‘s mind world and it will be one of my top five books.
