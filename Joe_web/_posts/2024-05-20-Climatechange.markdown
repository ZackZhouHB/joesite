- Climate Change: Causes, Effects, and Solutions

Did you know that Earth's temperature has been rising for over a century? Research from NASA indicates that the average surface temperature of the Earth has risen by 1.8 degrees over the past 30 years. People refer to this as a "climate crisis," which poses a significant challenge for saving our planet. In this essay, I will explain the major causes of climate change, how it affects human life, and explore possible solutions to restore sustainability for future generations.

- Causes of Climate Change

Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrialization are the main causes of climate change.
Coal, oil, and gas have been the primary energy sources supporting transportation and construction for the past few decades. Every day, cars and factories consume fossil fuels, releasing carbon dioxide (CO2) that traps heat in the atmosphere, causing the planet to warm globally.
Deforestation, the cutting down of trees, also contributes to climate change. Trees absorb CO2, and when they are removed, more CO2 remains in the atmosphere, worsening the greenhouse effect. Industrial processes, such as manufacturing cement and steel, release additional greenhouse gases, further warming the planet.

- Effects of Climate Change

The increase in global temperatures impacts all living things in ecosystems. Recent research shows that ice in the Arctic and Antarctica has significantly melted compared to 200 years ago. Rising sea levels significantly threaten coastal cities, potentially causing devastating floods.
Wild animals such as penguins, polar bears, and seals are seeing their habitats and food sources permanently altered. Scientists predict that some species living in the North Pole may become extinct in a few years. Marine life, including fish and coral reefs, also faces challenges in finding suitable environments due to changing ocean temperatures and acidity.

- Solutions to Climate Change

Modern civilization heavily relies on oil and coal, so sustainable solutions are necessary to ensure future generations can live on this beautiful planet without depleting resources or damaging the environment.
Despite the urgent situation, many countries, scientists, and universities are continuously developing and applying new energy solutions.
For example, Japan recently implemented policies requiring half of all new car registrations to be electric vehicles. The Australian government offers rewards for new homes that install solar panels as a renewable energy source. In China, over the past ten years, 1 million trees have been planted in the northern region to combat deforestation.
With the combined efforts of each country, community, and individual, people are making a difference in addressing climate change.

- Conclusion

In conclusion, climate change has been a major issue for centuries, and it will become a disaster if no action is taken worldwide. It is time for us all to work together to protect the planet for future generations.
