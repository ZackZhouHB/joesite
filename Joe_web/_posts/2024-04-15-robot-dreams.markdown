<b>Robot Dreams review:</b>


A dog lived alone in New York city, dreaming about friends. Everything changed when he bought a robot companion from an advertisement. The two quickly became inseparable friends. 

One day, robot was broken at the ocean beach, and the dog tried everyway but still had to leave his friend there until the beach reopens next year. 

The robot had 3 dreams to be reunited with the dog, unfortunately he was taken and smashed in the scrapyard. In the meantime, the dog struggled to make new friends and eventually bought another yellow robot.

Luckily, the robot was picked and repaired by a raccoon, and they developed new relationship soon. 

One afternoon, the robot spotted dog again, he decided to keep all their good memory and move on to each other's new life. 

I love this story's dialogue-free, emotional scenes and music, which made me cry. It reminded me another movie "Big hero 6", a robot companion Baymax. I really like the song "Semptember" from this movie.

"Robot Dreams" talks about friendship, love and loss, it will inspire anyone who had the same experience from the story and encourage people to grow up and move on when they get separated. 

I think robot dreams is one of my favorite movie, I will rate it 7.8/10.
