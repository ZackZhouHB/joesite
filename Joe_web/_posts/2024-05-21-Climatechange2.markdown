<b>If I am the king - Joe's original version</b>

Today I am going to announce three crucial decisions as the king of my country. The arrangements all support the solutions to climate change, a crisis that still existed for over a century.
 
First, I order builders and construction workers to plant twenty to fifty trees after they deforest them. Larger quantities of tree-cutting bring larger amounts of reforestation and anybody who doesn’t participate will be fined over 2000 dollars. The second time, their tax will increase by multiples of two. The last time they will be sent to prison.

The second announcement is to industrialize more electric cars than brands without renewable energy. In 2025, half of all car factories will produce electronic vehicles. In the following two years, 90% of all cars in my country have to be powered by sustainable power. In another 12 months, every single vehicle must be driven by clean energy.

Finally, houses powered by renewable energy sources like solar power or have just equipped them will be paid $500 every month. If people’s vehicles are powered by sustainable energy, they will receive $200 per every 3 to 4 weeks.

After I have secured this pattern in my country, I will hold a climate change meeting with other Kings and prime ministers from all over the globe to discuss the major causes of climate change, its effects and develop a list of policies and registrations that every country will follow in the future.

I hope my plan will stop climate change from spreading across the world and the atmosphere which can cause significant changes in habitat and diet in wildlife. Everyone needs to participate in my rules and policies to protect our planet for future generations.

<b>If I am the king - Dad's version</b>

Today, I am going to announce three crucial decisions as the king of this country, aiming to address our challenges of climate change, a crisis that has existed for over a century.
 

First, I request builders and construction companies to plant twenty to fifty trees for every area of deforestation they cause. Anyone who doesn’t apply will face a 2000-dollar fine. Repeat offenders will be triple fined. For third-time offences, they will be imprisoned.

The following announcement is about renewable energy-powered cars. In 2025, electronic vehicles must be half of the new car production and sales. By 2027, we will achieve 90% of new car sales with sustainable power in the country. By the end of 2030, we will stop any traditional petrol and diesel car registration.

Finally, houses powered by renewable energy sources like solar power or those planning to install them will receive a $500 rebate from the government green energy program. Any vehicle powered by sustainable energy will have its owner eligible to claim a $200 subsidy annually.

In addition, I plan to initiate a yearly climate change conference, inviting other leaders worldwide to discuss the major strategies to address climate crisis and enforce a list of agreements and regulations for all countries to follow.

Above measures will be signed and take effect next month. They are part of our commitment to improve and protect our country and the planet for future generations.

Once these measures are in place domestically, I will convene a global climate change summit with other kings and prime ministers to discuss the major causes and effects of climate change. We will develop a comprehensive list of policies and regulations for all countries to follow.

I hope my plan will help halt the spread of climate change and mitigate its impact on wildlife habitats and diets. It is imperative that everyone participates in these initiatives to protect our planet for future generations.
